How this test was worked out

Five steps, run on 18 Sep in 4.2 seconds

Markwise ran these steps in order. Where it was not sure enough to decide alone, it stopped and handed the item to you.

  1. Intake

    Read 96 answers from 12 answer sheets and matched each to its question in the marking scheme.

  2. Marker

    Marked every answer against the scheme as a draft. 96 draft marks, none of them final until you approve them.

    Stopped on 0 items for low marking confidence.

  3. Diagnostician

    Named the mistake behind each lost mark and pointed at the exact words in the answer as evidence. 31 mistakes named.

    Stopped on 3 items where the mathematics was right but the writing was not, and handed them to you.

  4. Cohort analyst

    Counted each mistake across the class. One pattern reached 5 of 12 students, above the 40% threshold, so it became a whole-class problem.

  5. Planner

    Proposed 21 actions, scored each, and filled your 120 minutes in priority order. 10 scheduled, 11 left out and listed with the reason.
