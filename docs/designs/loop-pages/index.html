LOOP page templates

Three ways to separate the five LOOP pages. The LMS sidebar and top bar are the same in all three. Use the sidebar inside each template to move between pages.

1. Bounded panelsEvery section is its own titled white box on a grey ground, and the summary rail stays on the left. 2. Banded surfaceNo boxes. Full-width bands, each with its title in a left column, and the figures in one strip at the top. 3. One thing at a timeEach page is a short list of numbered steps. Only one step shows at a time, with Back and Next.
